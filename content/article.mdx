## What X released

X published a public folder of code that, in the repository’s own words, is the core code that decides which posts a viewer sees in the For You feed. That is a statement about this folder, not a promise that you can turn on X’s live feed from your laptop.

/>

The README names the folders in that drop. Here is that map in everyday words.

**Putting a feed together.** `home-mixer` is the folder that builds the For You list: the steps, the scoring weights, and the calls to other systems. `candidate-pipeline` is the toolkit of step types that mixer is built on.

**Finding posts.** `thunder` keeps recent posts from accounts a viewer follows. `phoenix` looks up posts that sit near the viewer as lists of numbers, and also ranks them. `simclusters` groups accounts and posts by who engages with what, then uses those groups to find more posts. `phoenix-rankall` keeps the lookup list Phoenix searches. `phoenix-rankall-strato` decides which list a post belongs on.

**Ranking and variety.** Phoenix ranking guesses how likely a viewer is to act on a post. `vm-ranker` then reorders already-scored posts so neighbors are a bit less alike.

**Labels, hide, and show.** `visibility-filtering` answers allow, cover, or drop. `grox` looks at a post as it is published for things like spam, adult content, and violent media. `media-model-proxy` and `clip` are the image and video scoring pieces those labels use. `agatha`, `bdsm`, `user-cred-v2`, `adult-content`, and `pnsfwmedia` score accounts or media. `scarecrow`, `botmaker`, and `botmaker-rules` apply written label rules. `abuse-enforcement-service` can label, challenge, or suspend an account. `safety-label-user-agg` rolls post labels up to the account. `under-the-hood` builds the public label report.
